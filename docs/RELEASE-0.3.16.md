# NASAQ 0.3.16 — curated radio and quiet notifications

- Discover 18 selected stations, including Teletext, Radio Swiss Classic/Jazz,
  Venice Classic and SomaFM selections. Search and filter by musical style and
  suggested venue: offices, restaurants, cafes, shops, hotels and relaxation.
- Save favorite stations. Play or add without playing. Reopening a selected
  station reuses its existing playlist entry instead of duplicating it.
- Routine information, warning, error and confirmation dialogs are visual-only.
  Windows notification preferences are rendered with NASAQ's silent visual popup.
- Music, advertisements, intentional chimes and optional adhan audio keep their
  configured behavior. Prayer interruptions and licensing gates remain enforced.
- Includes the previous same-window file opening and duplicate prevention fixes.
- Live radio ignores seek/scan offsets and reconnects to the current broadcast
  when resuming after a pause.
- Adhan interruptions also silence radio while it is buffering and restore the
  interrupted station after the pause, without letting newly opened tracks bypass it.

Station suggestions describe musical mood; public-performance rights are not included.
