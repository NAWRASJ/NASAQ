NASAQ 0.3.7 — Business playback controls

Hold to talk: arm the microphone in Audio control, then hold the talk button.
Music gain becomes 5% of the selected volume. Releasing the button restores gain
smoothly over 1000 ms. Losing window focus or hiding the window releases paging.
STOP ALL cancels paging and the fade. The selected master volume is preserved.
Use an isolated PA output/headphones; an open microphone near speakers feeds back.

The player top bar shows the total playlist length prominently, remaining length,
and the shortage against business minutes (default 480; editable).
Unknown lengths mean the displayed total is a lower bound, not guaranteed coverage.
The Estimated end column uses the PC clock, current playback position and row order.
It updates once per second, including seeks, pauses and reordering. Midnight shows
the date. During pause it estimates resuming now. It does not forecast future
announcements, prayer breaks, crossfade overlap or unknown-length streams.
Shuffle, special repeat modes, queue overrides or skipped tracks suppress ETAs.
Drag tracks within the playlist to reorder; native undo/redo is retained.

Prayer settings: enable the monthly schedule, choose city/calculation authority and
fetch the times. Pause music is enabled by default for 10 minutes (1–60 editable).
An adhan file is optional. Missing adhan files still pause music. Announcements
cannot override the prayer hold; paging remains available. Music resumes only if
it was playing before the hold; STOP ALL prevents automatic resumption.
An unexpired hold is persisted across restart. NASAQ must be open and awake.
Verify downloaded times against the local mosque before deployment.
